# Dispatch: R3 Game Design, Retention & Replayability

## Objective
Design evidence-based game mechanics to elevate Slime Ninja from a simple game jam slice mechanic into a sticky, high-retention arcade game suited for browser play, with realistic low-cost indie scope.

## Specific Focus Areas
1. Session Loop & Round Variability:
   - Micro-events (e.g. "Slime Rush", "Golden Slime", "Freeze / Slow-Mo", "Bomb Danger").
   - Wave pattern variations, spawn formations (arcs, staggered bursts, split spawns).
   - Rare modifiers and mutators.
2. Risk vs Reward Mechanics:
   - High-risk / high-reward targets (explosive vs splitting vs armored slimes).
   - Combo multiplier deepening: how to reward slice precision, simultaneous multiple cuts, streak preservation.
3. Lightweight Meta-Progression:
   - Soft currency ("Slime Essence / Gems") earned in runs.
   - Unlockable blades (different trail VFX, audio, particle effects, plus distinct subtle mechanical perks vs cosmetic-only).
   - Unlockable slime skins / backgrounds / dojo themes.
   - Badges & achievements (skill milestones, cumulative achievements).
4. Retention Drivers:
   - Daily login / streak rewards (Day 1 - Day 7 calendar).
   - Daily quests & bite-sized challenges (e.g. "Slice 50 blue slimes", "Achieve 5x combo").
   - Dynamic / adaptive difficulty (rubber-banding / skill-based pacing so newcomers aren't crushed and veterans aren't bored).
5. Taxonomy & Classification:
   - Explicitly categorize every proposed feature: Gameplay-altering (affects balance, physics, scoring) vs Purely Cosmetic (VFX, trails, skins, audio).
